Support & Deposits

Support our mission or make a deposit for your matchmaking service.


Make a Donation

As a Community Interest Company (CIC), we’re committed to serving the Catholic community. Your donations help us keep our services accessible and reach more singles seeking faithful relationships.

  • Cover Costs: Help us maintain our website, database, and communication tools.
  • Grow Community: Enable us to reach more Catholics and expand our services.
Make a Deposit

Once we’ve found you a match, you’ll receive an email to make a refundable deposit to secure your date booking.

  • Required only after match confirmation.
  • Secure your restaurant reservation.
  • Fully refundable after your meeting.

Other Ways to Help

  • Share our service with single Catholics in your community.
  • Pray for the success of our mission and the couples we serve.
  • Provide feedback to help us improve our services.
  • Share your success story if we helped you find your spouse.
