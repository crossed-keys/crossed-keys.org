Matchmaker Q&A

Choose Your Path
Thu 26th Nov 6:30pm
A seminar designed especially for Crossed Keys singles,
lead by Fr Anton Vogelsang, LC – a well-travelled priest (with a background in mathematics & software engineering) based in Rome – who frequently visits London to deliver advanced Bible study courses.
