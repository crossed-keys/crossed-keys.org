Privacy Policy

Crossed Keys CIC

Your data is handled according to the four categories below:

Category Your data
Public: visible by anyone –
Blind: shared with a blind date After your meeting and only if you both agree: phone
Protected: might be shared with other logged-in verified users of the appropriate city, gender and age range ⭐️ Self-description & ideal spouse description
Private: only visible by matchmaking admins Anything else you provide us with: email, name, age, photo, etc.

Crossed Keys is a CIC. There are no investors or shareholders, nor do we sell your data. No ads; no cookie banners.

We may use fully self-hosted AI (no data is sent to third-parties) to help find matches for you, but we treat these suggestions as mildly helpful & error-prone. All decisions are made by our team of humans, as per the Vatican’s stance on AI.

We do not keep backups. If you delete your profile, we cannot recover it.
