About Us

Crossed Keys CIC

Our Mission

We support singles who are ready to meet to pursue the Catholic definition of marriage. We believe courtship should be guided by clarity, virtue, and the teachings of the Church – rather than by secular dating, hookup culture, and endless swiping. Our mission is to create meaningful introductions between Catholics who desire commitment, family life, and holiness.

Why we Started

After witnessing countless Catholics struggle with dating apps (prioritising appearance over virtue, and entertainment over commitment), we knew there had to be a better way. We started Crossed Keys CIC to provide a service which respects the dignity of each person in alignment with sacramental marriage.

Our Approach

Our matchmaking services combine traditional values with modern convenience, creating a safe space where Catholics can meet others who are serious about building intentional, Christ-centred relationships.

The Team

Matchmakers

Our team of matchmakers are married Catholics who understand the importance of faith in relationships. They take the time to get to know you personally, ensuring that introductions are thoughtful and aligned with your individual profile.

Advisors

Clergy and lay professionals provide spiritual & practical guidance to ensure our services align with Church teachings and legal requirements.

They also offer free workshops, resources, and advice.

Leadership

Crossed Keys is a registered CIC based in London, UK.

There are no investors or shareholders, nor affiliation to any particular Catholic order/dioscese/parish.
