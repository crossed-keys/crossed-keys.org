About Us

Our Mission

Crossed Keys CIC exists to support singles who are ready to meet to pursue the Catholic definition of marriage. We believe courtship should be guided by clarity, virtue, and the teachings of the Church – rather than by secular dating culture or endless swiping. Our mission is to create meaningful introductions between Catholics who desire commitment, family life, and holiness.

The Team

Matchmakers

Our team of matchmakers are married Catholics who understand the importance of faith in relationships. They take the time to get to know each client personally, ensuring that introductions are thoughtful and aligned with each individual’s profile.

Advisors

Clergy and lay professionals provide spiritual & practical guidance to ensure our services align with Church teachings and legal requirements.

They also offer free workshops, resources, and advice.

Leadership
