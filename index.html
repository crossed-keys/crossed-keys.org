Crossed Keys CIC

London Catholic Matchmaking

This is not a dating app nor a social network. We aim to minimise your time on this site.

This is a traditional matchmaking service which just happens to have an online interface.

We bring together Catholics who want to place God at the centre of their marriage, keeping in mind teachings such as
1 Cor 7, Eph 5, Sir 25-26, Mat 19, Heb 13, Mat 5, 1 Pet 3, CCC 1601-1666 [1, 2], and CCC 2331-2400 [3, 4].

On signing up to our blind or bespoke service, our team of matchmakers will look for someone who would make you a good future spouse.

Our Services

Blind Matchmaking

We give you a time and location. You bring yourself.

Every quarter, we run a round of blind matchmaking in London, UK.

Login and fill in your profile. Your profile is reviewed and matched anonymously with another compatible Catholic.

Our team of matchmakers uses your profile to find a match with someone else in our database, and we make an anonymous restaurant reservation for both of you. After meeting, you let us know whether you would like us to share your contact details on your behalf.

Bespoke Matchmaking

We schedule a call with you and organise matches without the wait.

A personalised, human-led matchmaking experience. You’ll work directly with our matchmakers to define your vision, and will receive curated introductions to potential matches with Catholics ready for a sacramental relationship.

We use our entire online & in-person networks to find a match, discussing and working with you to find a spouse.
